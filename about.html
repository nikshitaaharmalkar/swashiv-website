Our Story

Ready to Wear, Ready to Shine

The idea behind Swashiv, and the person behind it.

The Designer

Suvidha Harmalkar

Swashiv Fashion is run by Suvidha Harmalkar out of Mapusa, Goa. What started as a small edit of ethnic co-ord sets has grown into a considered collection spanning festive wear and easy casual-to-office pieces — all built around one belief: getting dressed shouldn't be complicated.

Every piece is chosen to be ready to wear straight out of the packet, so effortless style is never more than a message away.

What We Stand For

Three things Swashiv doesn't compromise on

Made to be worn easily

Ready-to-wear means exactly that — no alterations needed, no second-guessing.

Handpicked, not mass-produced

Each set is chosen individually, so the collection stays considered, not overwhelming.

Honest, accessible pricing

₹500–₹2,000 a piece — priced to be worn often, not saved for one occasion.
